## Emotional Trade Journal

Use this to track what you're feeling during trades:

### During Entry
- [ ] Excited (FOMO - risky)
- [ ] Confident (good signal)
- [ ] Scared (second-guessing)
- [ ] Calm (best state)

### During Loss
- [ ] Angry (revenge trading - avoid)
- [ ] Sad (give up immediately)
- [ ] Calm (take stop, move on)
- [ ] Learning (analyzing why)

### During Win
- [ ] Greedy (hold too long)
- [ ] Happy (secure profit)
- [ ] Overconfident (double up - avoid)
- [ ] Satisfied (take it and go)

**Example Entry:**
```
Date: Sept 17, 2026
Trade: NVDA (Loss -£23.75)
Before: Calm, confident signal
During: Got scared at -£10, wanted to exit
After: Angry at myself for exiting emotionally
Lesson: I'm reactive to small losses. Need to journal the REASON I took signal
        to remember it when scared.
```

---

## Key Rules to Remember While Logging

1. **Log immediately after exit**
   - Don't wait (memory fades)
   - While trade is fresh, note the feel

2. **Be honest**
   - "Emotional exit" not "market turned"
   - "Ignored volume check" not "volume wasn't important"

3. **Find patterns**
   - After 20 trades, do you lose on one stock?
   - Do you win in certain time of day?
   - Do emotional trades lose more?

4. **Use this to improve**
   - Logs aren't just records
   - They're feedback to refine the system
   - Better logging = faster improvement

---

## Example: Using Logs to Improve

**From logs after 20 trades:**
```
Pattern 1: Win rate 42% overall, but 65% on tech stocks (AAPL, MSFT, NVDA)
→ Action: Weight watchlist 70% tech, 30% other

Pattern 2: Lost 5/7 trades between 11am-1pm (early close winners often)
→ Action: Stop trading 11am-1pm, focus early morning & late afternoon

Pattern 3: Every emotional exit lost money (5 trades, all losses)
→ Action: Removed emotion - only exit at stop or target, set auto-alerts

Result: Next 20 trades = 58% win rate (vs 42%)
```

**This is how you go from 51% improvement to 2x improvement.**
